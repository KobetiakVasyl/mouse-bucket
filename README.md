# MouseBucket

A desktop app for Linux and Windows for uploading files to Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage and Google Drive, and downloading files from them, with your own keys. Azure and Google Drive are in beta. There's no account and no backend: the app talks to each platform directly, and your keys stay on your computer, encrypted by the operating system.

Save a place in the cloud and its keys as a template once, then pick the template to upload files to its folder, or to browse it and download a file.
